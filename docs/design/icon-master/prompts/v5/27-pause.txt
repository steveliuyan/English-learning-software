STYLE CONTRACT v5 - CHUNKY STICKER (identical across the whole set, derived from the supplied reference sheet):
Chunky die-cut sticker illustration of a single friendly object, slightly dimensional like a soft moulded clay toy flattened onto the page. Rounded simplified geometry. Every form is a SOLID opaque colour mass filled with ONE perfectly flat tone - zero shading and zero gradient inside a mass - with crisp clean edges. No translucency, no mesh gradient, no texture, no raster noise.

DEPTH RULE: each icon has exactly ONE mid-tone main mass, exactly ONE darker same-hue mass that reads as a side wall, an underside or a recessed inner area, and ONE bold DARKEST same-hue contour that runs around the whole silhouette and around every opening. The contour is thick and confident, about 4 to 5 percent of the icon width, uniform in weight, with rounded joins and rounded ends - a structural outline, not a thin accent line. Any interior detail line uses the same thick contour weight.

HIGHLIGHT RULE: exactly ONE glossy near-white highlight patch, placed on the upper left of the largest mass, shaped like a short comma, crescent or oval, following the curve of that mass. It sits fully inside the mass and never touches the contour. No other white anywhere in the icon.

COLOUR RULE: all solids of an icon belong to ONE hue family: mid tone, darker shade, darkest contour. Gentle slightly desaturated pastel colours, bright enough to stay legible at 17 px. No neon, no pure black.

FRAMING: a single icon, centered, occupying about 70 percent of the frame, on a plain very light neutral off-white background (#F6F9F6), generous even padding.

NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO labels, NO logos, NO watermark, NO outer border, NO rounded-square tile behind the icon, NO cast shadow on the background, NO gradient shading, NO photorealism - the icon floats directly on the flat background.

ICON SUBJECT:
Two thick vertical rounded bars standing side by side with a gap between them. The left bar is the mid-tone main mass; the right bar is the darker mass; the darkest contour runs around both bars; one glossy near-white comma highlight sits on the upper left of the left bar.

THIS ICON'S COLOUR FAMILY: mid-tone main mass #6CCBAB , darker mass and thick contour #1A7A63 , single glossy highlight near-white #F4FBF7 .
